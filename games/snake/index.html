How to play Neon Snake

Guide the snake to the food. Each bite makes it one segment longer and a little faster. The game ends if the snake hits a wall or its own tail.

Controls: Arrows/WASD steer, Space/P pause, M mute; swipe or d-pad on touch

Tips

  • Stay near the edges early and leave the middle open for later.
  • Plan two turns ahead: know where your tail will be when you get to the food.
  • When the snake is long, sweep the board in rows so you never trap yourself.

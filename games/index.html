Free retro browser games

Small arcade and puzzle games. No downloads, no sign-up, no ads. Click one and play.

  • Neon Snake
    Steer a glowing snake around the grid, eat to grow, and survive as the speed keeps climbing.
  • Brick Breaker
    Bounce the ball off your paddle to smash neon brick walls across ever-tougher levels with just three lives.
  • Block Drop
    Rotate and stack falling blocks to clear lines, watch the next piece, and level up as the drop speeds up.
  • 2048 Merge
    Slide numbered tiles on a 4x4 board and merge matching pairs until you reach 2048.
  • Neon Mines
    Clear the grid without hitting a mine, using the numbers to work out where they hide.
  • Neon Memory
    Flip glowing neon cards two at a time and find every matching pair in as few moves as you can.

Which game should I play?

  • Five minutes to kill: Neon Snake or Brick Breaker. Quick rounds, instant restart.
  • Want to think: Neon Mines or 2048 Merge. Logic over reflexes, so plan every move.
  • Classic falling blocks: Block Drop. Clear lines, speed climbs as you level up.
  • Relaxed or playing with kids: Neon Memory. Flip cards and match pairs in as few moves as you can.

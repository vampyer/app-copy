How to play 2048 Merge

Slide every tile on the 4x4 board in one direction. Two tiles with the same number merge into one with double the value. Reach the 2048 tile to win.

Controls: Arrows/WASD slide, Space/P pause, N new game, M mute; swipe on touch

Tips

  • Pick one corner and keep your biggest tile there.
  • Use mostly two directions, and a third only when you must.
  • Build a chain of falling values along one edge (1024, 512, 256...).

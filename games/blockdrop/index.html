How to play Block Drop

Falling blocks of seven shapes drop into the well. Move and rotate them to fill complete rows; full rows clear and score points. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

Controls: Arrows move/rotate/soft drop, Enter hard drop, Space/P pause, M mute; swipe, tap or buttons on touch

Tips

  • Keep the surface flat and leave one column open for long pieces.
  • Check the next-piece preview before you place the current one.
  • Clearing several rows at once scores more than one at a time.

How to play Neon Memory

All cards start face down. Flip two at a time: if they show the same symbol they stay up, if not they flip back. Match every pair to win. Pick Easy (6 pairs), Normal (8 pairs) or Hard (12 pairs).

Controls: Tap or click to flip, arrows + Enter/Space on keyboard, N new game, M mute

Tips

  • Flip one card you know and one you have not seen yet, so every move teaches you something.
  • Say the symbols to yourself as you see them; it makes positions easier to remember.
  • Work through the board in rows instead of jumping around.

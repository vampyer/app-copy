FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first data source and send a report in under 15 minutes. Our templates do the heavy lifting, and you can customise everything later.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. You get full access to the Growth plan for 14 days. If you don't pick a plan, your account simply pauses — nothing is deleted.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing page. Changes are prorated to the day.

Will my clients see the Launch brand?

Not on Growth or Scale. Reports, emails and the client portal use your logo, colors and custom domain.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're SOC 2 Type II audited and never sell or share your data.

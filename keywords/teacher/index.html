Teacher Resume Keywords

Applicant tracking systems rank you by how closely your resume matches the job post. These are the terms that show up most in teacher postings. Use the ones that are true for you, in the same words the employer uses.

Skills

  • lesson planning
  • classroom management
  • differentiated instruction
  • curriculum development
  • student assessment
  • parent communication
  • IEP
  • formative assessment
  • small group instruction
  • behavior management
  • data-driven instruction
  • student engagement

Tools and certifications

  • Google Classroom
  • Canvas
  • SMART Board
  • state teaching license

Example bullet

Raised 5th-grade reading proficiency from 58% to 79% in one year through small group instruction.

The pattern: action verb, what you did, a number that proves it. Put 2 or 3 keywords in your summary line, 8 to 12 in a plain Skills line, and the rest inside bullets like this one.

How to use this list in 10 minutes

  1. Open the job post you are applying to and highlight every skill and tool it names.
  2. Compare it with the list above. Anything both share is a priority keyword.
  3. Paste your resume into the checker below to see what is missing.
  4. Add the missing terms you can honestly claim, using the employer's exact wording.
  5. Save as .docx or a text-based PDF with no tables, columns or text boxes.

Customer Service Representative Resume Keywords

Applicant tracking systems rank you by how closely your resume matches the job post. These are the terms that show up most in customer service representative postings. Use the ones that are true for you, in the same words the employer uses.

Skills

  • customer satisfaction
  • issue resolution
  • de-escalation
  • call handling
  • order processing
  • returns and refunds
  • first call resolution
  • service level agreements
  • product knowledge
  • upselling
  • complaint handling
  • data entry

Tools and certifications

  • Zendesk
  • Salesforce
  • Freshdesk
  • CRM
  • Microsoft Office

Example bullet

Resolved 60+ customer calls per day with a 92% first call resolution rate and a 4.8/5 CSAT score.

The pattern: action verb, what you did, a number that proves it. Put 2 or 3 keywords in your summary line, 8 to 12 in a plain Skills line, and the rest inside bullets like this one.

How to use this list in 10 minutes

  1. Open the job post you are applying to and highlight every skill and tool it names.
  2. Compare it with the list above. Anything both share is a priority keyword.
  3. Paste your resume into the checker below to see what is missing.
  4. Add the missing terms you can honestly claim, using the employer's exact wording.
  5. Save as .docx or a text-based PDF with no tables, columns or text boxes.
